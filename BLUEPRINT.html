Finance Exception Investigator

Status: specification and synthetic fixtures only. Application implementation has not started. No application tests, benchmark runs, customer outcomes or savings are claimed.

A planned local investigator of invoice, purchase-order, payment and ledger mismatches. Deterministic calculations establish amounts and exceptions; optional AI proposes explicitly labeled, record-linked explanations.

01 — Product requirements

Status: proposed v1 requirements; none is an achieved product capability.

Problem, users and outcome

A controller sees records that disagree and must reconstruct what happened. The product should assemble the relevant records, calculate differences, identify missing evidence and prepare a reviewable case.

The primary user is a finance operations analyst investigating mismatches. The controller owns the review outcome. A finance systems engineer imports exports and diagnoses import problems. One local operator may perform these roles in the demonstration; this does not establish enterprise identity or segregation of duties.

The outcome is an evidence packet and recorded human decision. The initial product hypothesis is that contextual explanations can improve diagnosis beyond deterministic matching. Buyer demand and human time savings remain untested.

Scope and accounting convention

One synthetic purchase-to-payment workflow, USD only, amounts in integer cents. A bundle contains related orders, invoices, payments and simplified ledger entries as of a fixed date. Invoice and payment ledger entries represent positive source amounts, not a complete double-entry general ledger. Taxes are included in document totals. One invoice references one order; one payment applies to one invoice; multiple payments may settle an invoice.

Cover duplicate invoice records, amount discrepancies, overdue partial payments and missing supporting records. A legitimate partial payment before the due date is a clean open balance, not automatically an exception. Invoice date, due date and as-of date govern this distinction.

Exclude exchange conversion, tax calculation, credit notes, refunds, netting, payment allocation across invoices, document OCR, real bank connections, ERP mutations and autonomous accounting decisions. Unsupported inputs fail validation or require review; silently approximating them is prohibited.

Functional requirements and stories

ID User story and required behavior Acceptance evidence to build
FR-01 As an engineer, I import four CSV files or a bundle JSON and receive row-level validation errors before any invalid bundle is committed. Valid JSON/CSV equivalent; missing headers, malformed dates, decimals and foreign currency rejected.
FR-02 As an analyst, I trust balances because calculations use bounded integer minor units. Exact totals, outstanding balance and difference values; no float conversion.
FR-03 As an engineer, I can repeat an import or investigation without duplicate records or contradictory results. Same content returns the same stored identity; conflicting content under the same bundle ID is rejected.
FR-04 As an analyst, I see candidate and accepted links, the rule used and ambiguity. Same-supplier reference matching, one-to-many partial payments and ambiguous candidates tested.
FR-05 As an analyst, I see duplicate invoice evidence without counting it twice as a payable. Same supplier plus normalized invoice number raises a duplicate finding; conflicting duplicates remain unresolved.
FR-06 As an analyst, I see exact order/invoice, invoice/ledger and payment/ledger differences. Nonzero differences beyond the configured tolerance have input-field citations and a calculation.
FR-07 As an analyst, I distinguish clean open balances, fully settled invoices and overdue shortfalls. Clean future-due partial payment and overdue variant produce different outcomes.
FR-08 As an analyst, I see missing orders, invoices or required ledger records and a specific request for evidence. No invented missing record or assumed zero; unpaid but not-due invoice does not require a payment record.
FR-09 As a reviewer, I open the evidence supporting every substantive claim. Record IDs and field pointers resolve; derived claims cite deterministic calculations; unsupported hypotheses are labeled.
FR-10 As an analyst, I optionally obtain grounded hypotheses without letting AI override calculations or clear a case. Invalid output, missing citations and timeout fall back to the deterministic report.
FR-11 As a controller, I record acceptance, an evidence-backed dismissal or a request for evidence. Immutable review event records actor, reason, evidence and report version; unresolved findings cannot disappear.
FR-12 As a reviewer, I inspect a case queue and evidence timeline using keyboard navigation. Queue filters, empty/error/loading states, ordered evidence and usable narrow-screen layout.
FR-13 As a reviewer, I export the exact case I reviewed. JSON plus printable HTML include versions, findings, evidence, human events and synthetic-data label.
FR-14 As an operator, I receive explicit failure states and bounded optional model spend. Invalid rows, interrupted work, malicious text, provider failure and budget exhaustion tested.
FR-15 As an evaluator, I compare strategies using identical sanitized inputs without answer or source-name leakage. Opaque projection, ancestry-separated splits, 800 runs and label-leakage tests; complete scoring.
FR-16 As a new user, I reproduce the demonstration from documented setup. Fresh local environment, locked dependencies, example commands and report artifacts verified.

Workflow and screen specification

  1. Import: choose CSV directory or JSON; display bundle count, currency, as-of date and errors. Confirm the synthetic demonstration label.
  2. Queue: columns: case ID, supplier, invoice references, deterministic outcome, open balance, exception types, review state. Filters: outcome, exception type and review state. Search by record/reference. Always show the total and filtered counts.
  3. Case: header contains bundle/report versions and as-of date. Main column shows findings, exact calculations and ordered record events. Side panel shows AI hypotheses separately, evidence gaps and reviewer controls.
  4. Evidence drawer: opens the source record, highlights a field and shows file/pointer or CSV row. Missing evidence displays the expected record type and reference, never a fabricated citation.
  5. Review/export: reason and cited evidence required for dismissals; confirm the local review event; export a versioned snapshot.

Use text labels as well as color. Show amounts as USD with two decimal places while retaining cents in exports. Never label a reviewed case “paid” unless records substantiate payment. A human acknowledgment closes an investigation task, not a ledger balance.

Interface decisions

Use a local server-rendered interface. AI defaults off. Avoid uncalibrated confidence percentages.

Data contracts and ingestion

Each input has schema version, bundle ID, template family, lineage group, as-of date, USD currency and four arrays. Every record has a stable ID, supplier ID and source locator. Orders contain order number, date and total; invoices contain invoice number, order reference, issue/due dates and total; payments contain payment reference, optional invoice reference, date and total; ledger entries identify invoice/payment kind, relevant reference, date and total.

CSV files are orders.csv, invoices.csv, payments.csv and ledger_entries.csv. Their headers match the supplied CSV example, with bundle ID, template family, lineage group, as-of and currency repeated. The importer creates source locators from actual filenames and physical row numbers, including the header as row 1. It must not trust a caller-provided CSV provenance field. Empty files still require headers. Reject mismatched bundle metadata.

Store source bytes with a digest and normalized records with stable IDs. Normalize text using Unicode NFC, surrounding-whitespace removal and case folding for matching; preserve original text, punctuation and leading zeros. Reject duplicate record IDs. Unique record IDs do not imply unique invoices.

Dates are ISO calendar dates. All event dates must be on or before as-of; a due date may be later but must not precede issue date. All monetary fields are integers between 1 and 1,000,000,000,000 cents. Derived totals must remain within signed 64-bit storage bounds; reject overflow before persistence.

Evaluated input boundary

An evaluator-owned allowlist converts archives to strategy-input.schema.json before every strategy, including exact and rules. Replace bundle IDs, record IDs and source locators with opaque random tokens independent of outcomes. Preserve mapping consistency and business references. Exclude descriptive filenames, paths, template_family, lineage_group, partition/split, category labels, oracle fields and comments. Retain necessary business facts.

All strategies receive identical projected bytes. Model context may add only calculations/findings derived from those bytes; derived finding types are not oracle labels. Strategies/models cannot read reverse maps, archives or truth. Citations use opaque IDs and field pointers. After scoring, the evaluator joins its reverse map to render readable source citations. Never feed enriched UI/export output back into evaluation.

Matching and calculations

Exact baseline: raw same-supplier references only; exact amount checks; no inferred link when a reference is absent. Rules baseline: normalized references, duplicate checks, partial-payment aggregation and defined fallback candidates. Both use the same authoritative arithmetic.

Accept an invoice-to-order link only when its order reference identifies one order for the supplier. Link payment-to-invoice by unique reference; multiple payments may link to one invoice. For a missing payment invoice reference, a rules candidate must share supplier, exactly match the invoice amount, and fall within 14 days after issue and before as-of. Accept only a unique candidate. No fuzzy name-only match and no arbitrary tie-breaking. Candidate ambiguity requires review.

Link invoice ledger entries by supplier and invoice reference, and payment ledger entries by supplier and payment reference. Require exactly one corresponding ledger entry per unique invoice and per payment in v1; multiple ledger entries remain an ambiguity because split journal entries are outside scope.

Calculate outstanding = invoice total minus sum of uniquely linked payments. A negative result is an overpayment discrepancy. Zero is settled. Positive with as-of after due date is an overdue partial/unpaid exception; otherwise it is a clean open balance if other checks pass. Do not aggregate duplicate invoice faces into liability. Conflicting duplicates prevent clearance.

Check invoice against order total, invoice ledger against invoice total and payment ledger against payment total. Default tolerance is zero; any configured nonzero tolerance is visible and versioned. Clean partial payments do not need each payment to equal the invoice total.

Persistence and idempotency

Proposed tables: bundles, records, runs, links, calculations, findings, claims, review_events and exports. Foreign keys connect every child to its parent. Use parameterized SQL, explicit transactions and connection-level foreign-key enforcement.

Bundle identity is bundle ID plus a canonical-content digest. Identical re-import is a no-op; changed content under an existing ID is a conflict, requiring a new bundle version. A deterministic run key hashes bundle digest, strategy, configuration and rules version. For AI, also include provider/model snapshot, prompt version and trial ID; repeated trial IDs reuse the result. Different trial IDs intentionally rerun.

A review action has a client idempotency key unique within report/actor. Repeat returns the existing event. Events append; concurrent stale report updates return conflict. No destructive edit of the underlying evidence is part of review.

AI boundary, evidence and failures

The model receives only sanitized records and their deterministic links, calculations and findings. It cannot access expected-outcome files, filesystem tools or posting functions. Treat every source string as untrusted data. Supply a fixed output contract and reject unknown fields.

Claims have kind fact, hypothesis or missing_evidence. Facts cite existing record fields or calculation IDs; citations must resolve and calculations must agree. Hypotheses say what could explain a finding and what would discriminate it; a citation alone does not establish that interpretation is true. Missing-evidence claims name a needed source and cite the observed reference creating that need.

The deterministic outcome is clear, review_required or insufficient_evidence. A processing failure produces failed. AI may add hypotheses and evidence requests; it cannot change an outcome to clear, remove findings, or authorize a posting. The result records unavailable/failed AI status distinctly from completed AI output.

Proposed local HTTP contract

GET /cases lists paginated metadata; GET /cases/{id} retrieves a report; GET /cases/{id}/records/{record_id} resolves evidence; POST /imports validates local uploads; POST /cases/{id}/reviews appends a review; GET /cases/{id}/export returns a snapshot. Reject oversized input, unsafe filenames and unknown records. No endpoint mutates a bank, ERP or ledger.

Use timeouts, at most one retry for transient provider errors, and a per-run token/cost ceiling. On failure keep the deterministic report and record the reason. Do not save secrets or raw provider request bodies in public logs.

04 — Evaluation protocol

Status: preregistration draft. No measured result exists.

Research question and hypotheses

Does rules-plus-AI investigation improve evidence-backed diagnosis over deterministic reconciliation without increasing incorrect clearances?

H1: AI hypotheses increase correct supported diagnoses among exception bundles. H2: this can occur without increasing false clearance or unsupported factual claims. These hypotheses may fail. Because the proposed AI cannot override deterministic clearance, equal clearance outcomes between rules and rules-plus-AI are expected by construction; that equality is not evidence that free-form agents are safe.

The benchmark measures seeded synthetic problems under declared conventions. It cannot establish accounting assurance, real-world loss prevention, compliance or human time saved.

Corpus and split

Create 200 original bundles: 40 development and 160 held out. The 40 development bundles include the eight supplied seeds; do not count the CSV rendering of the clean seed twice. The remaining 32 and all 160 held-out bundles are future work.

Development target: 20 exception and 20 clean bundles. Held-out target: 80 exception and 80 clean. Allocate held-out exceptions as 20 duplicate invoices, 20 amount discrepancies, 20 overdue partial/unpaid balances and 20 missing-record cases. Include clean partial payments among the 80 clean. Each exception bundle has one primary category; record additional effects separately to avoid double-counting.

Nominal generation targets are 20 development and 80 held-out template families, usually two bundles per family. These are planning targets, not independent-sample counts or guaranteed power. The eight supplied seeds share one lineage. Identify highest shared ancestry before splitting: variants, templates and descendants stay together regardless of family names. Review independence, report actual lineage counts, and adjust template construction within the fixed 40/160 totals. Random row splitting is prohibited. Vary dates, amounts, reference noise and record ordering without encoding outcomes in metadata.

An evaluator maintains expected links, exception category, correct outcome, diagnosis concepts and required evidence. Every strategy receives only the strict sanitized projection: opaque bundle/record/source IDs, no readable filenames, family/lineage/split/category labels or truth. Evaluator-only reverse mappings resolve citations after scoring. Keep archives, mappings and truth outside strategy/model access. Have a second reviewer validate the held-out oracle before freezing; log ambiguous cases and resolve them without looking at model outputs.

Seal evaluator-only manifests containing IDs, ancestry, projection version and source/projected hashes. Record generation seed, schema version and protocol revision. A held-out set is evaluated once after tuning. After results inform changes, call it a validation set and create a new test set for a new confirmatory claim.

Three comparators and execution count

Strategy Behavior
exact Literal same-supplier reference matching and exact checks; abstains on missing/ambiguous links.
rules Normalization, duplicate detection, deterministic invariants, partial balances and documented unique fallback matching.
rules_ai Identical rules output plus model hypotheses and evidence requests. No hidden extra records or ground-truth access.

Give all strategies identical input bundles and calculation definitions. Freeze rules, tolerance, prompt, allowed model and cost ceiling on development data. Report different actual work and costs; never imply equal cost because inputs match.

Run exact once and rules once across 160 held-out bundles: 320 runs. Run rules_ai three independent trial IDs per bundle: 480 runs. Total: 800, excluding development, spend pilots and optional sensitivity checks. Record failed/time-out runs in the denominator. Seeds, temperature and model settings are logged where supported; model nondeterminism may remain.

Metrics with explicit denominators

Metric Definition
Link precision / recall Correct accepted record-pair links divided by all accepted links / all oracle links; also report ambiguous abstentions.
Exception recall Exception bundles explicitly flagged with the correct primary category divided by 80. Missing-evidence flags count only for matching oracle cases.
Missed-exception rate Exception bundles without the correct exception flag divided by 80; includes failed runs and unclassified abstentions.
False-clearance rate Exception bundles returned clear divided by 80. Report three AI trial rates separately plus the proportion with any false clearance.
Clean-case false alarm Clean bundles flagged review_required or insufficient_evidence divided by 80. Report failed runs separately.
Clearance coverage All bundles returned clear divided by 160; also clean clearances divided by 80. Low false clearance by abstaining on everything is not success.
Diagnosis correctness Exception bundles with the required causal diagnosis supported by observed records divided by 80; audit rubric permits multiple valid phrasings.
Citation correctness Audited citations that resolve to the claimed record/field and support the associated factual statement divided by all audited citations. Report resolution and semantic support separately.
Unsupported-claim rate Unsupported factual claims divided by all audited factual claims; separately count uncited facts. Hypotheses remain audited for misleading certainty.
Cost / performance Actual tokens, priced API cost, retries, wall-clock duration and failure count per strategy; disclose price date/model.

Use paired bundle comparisons. Cluster uncertainty by highest shared lineage, nesting families, bundles and AI trials within it; descendants are not independent. Report actual independent-cluster counts and interval limitations. Publish per-category results and every false clearance. For zero observed errors, state the numerator and denominator; never claim zero real-world risk.

Human audit and reporting

Prespecify 40 held-out bundles, ten per exception category, selected by a recorded seed before execution. Audit all three AI trials for those bundles; a second reviewer independently audits the same claims. Additionally inspect all false clearances and any clean-case unsupported factual claims flagged by automated checks. Report disagreements and adjudications; if no independent reviewer is available, state that the external-audit gate is unmet.

Reject unexpected projection fields. Test renamed sources, shuffled opaque IDs and injected label canaries; business outcomes must be invariant after ID remapping. Inspect serialized strategy/model requests for leaks. The scorer validates pointers, IDs and arithmetic; semantic support needs judgment. Never use the evaluated model as the sole judge of its own explanations. Grader fixes require a documented reason and rescoring all affected strategies.

The report must include setup, provenance, baselines, frozen configuration, corpus distribution, failures, paired effects, uncertainty, cost, limitations and a conclusion even if AI provides no improvement.
