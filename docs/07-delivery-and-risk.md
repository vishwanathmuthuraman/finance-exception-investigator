# 07 — Technical limitations and research risks

This repository specifies an evidence-based reconciliation experiment. It has no implemented investigator, measured benchmark, operational deployment or demonstrated fraud-detection capability.

| Limitation | Consequence | Verification or disclosure required |
|---|---|---|
| Simplified purchase-to-payment records | The data is not a complete double-entry general ledger. | State supported record semantics; reject unsupported accounting situations. |
| Single-currency convention | Exchange, tax and allocation complexity is excluded. | Enforce explicit currency and exact minor-unit arithmetic. |
| Ambiguous record matching | Similar references do not prove two records belong together. | Separate candidates from accepted links and preserve unresolved cases. |
| Missing evidence | A missing document is not evidence of zero liability or wrongdoing. | Request evidence explicitly and prevent automatic false clearance. |
| Model explanations | Fluent text may contradict source records or calculations. | Validate citations, label hypotheses and retain deterministic findings on failure. |
| Correlated synthetic templates | Many cases may represent few independent situations. | Split and calculate uncertainty at the highest shared lineage. |
| Unrealistic prevalence | Balanced clean/exception cases do not estimate live alert precision. | Publish denominators and limits on operational generalization. |
| Reviewer and grader disagreement | A score can conceal ambiguous truth or inconsistent interpretation. | Preserve audit disagreements, corrections and negative results. |

A financial discrepancy can arise from timing, data quality or normal business behavior. This project must not describe an exception as proof of dishonesty, issue accounting judgments autonomously, or claim recovered money without separate evidence. It does not execute payments or modify financial systems.

Keep all starter bundles and future public examples synthetic. Report exact/rules/rules-plus-AI comparisons, failed runs and unsupported inputs. A completed research artifact and a product meeting proposed acceptance thresholds are separate claims.

See [evaluation](04-evaluation.md), [release gates](05-definition-of-done.md) and [publication rules](08-publication.md). Customer exports, credentials and commercial investigation notes belong outside the public repository.
